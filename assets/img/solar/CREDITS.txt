Planet textures in 2k/ and 1k/

Source: Solar System Scope, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0),
         https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Taken from the Wikimedia Commons copies of the 2k set
(File:Solarsystemscope texture 2k *.jpg / .png / .tif, verified by SHA-1).

Changes: resized (2k: 2048 px wide, 1k: 1024 px; the Moon, Venus, Uranus,
Neptune and the roughness map at half that) and converted to WebP. The
Earth roughness map is the specular map inverted and rescaled to 0.35-0.9;
the Saturn ring strip is the ring alpha map cut to 16 rows.

The page credits them under the "How to explore" notes on /solarsystem.


Moon and dwarf-planet maps in 2k/ and 1k/

Public domain (NASA, JPL, USGS and the missions named), from Wikimedia Commons,
verified by SHA-1, resized to 1024 x 512 (2k) and 512 x 256 (1k) and converted to WebP.
Where a mission never imaged part of a body, that part is filled from the colours
around it (a smooth blend towards the map's average, with light noise).
